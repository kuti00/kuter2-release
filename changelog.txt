Update 1.0.35
- New: Loot Crates! Bosses now drop Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary Loot Crates (in Legendary, Mythical, Fabled and Titan bags)
- Double-click a crate to spin for one prize: Fame, Shards, Fries, skins, slot unlockers, LG / MT / FB forge fragments, Astral Essence, companions and even Starseeds - items go to your Gift Chest
- Harder dungeons drop better crates - see "Loot Crates" in the Codex
- Luck Rush overflow: past level 5, every 10,000 XP earns a star (+10% loot each, up to 10 stars = +700%). Stars show before your name in chat, and a death takes them first
- Zenith Broadblade: tighter, simpler waves that stay close to the blade, damage rebalanced
- New: The Endless Arena! Arena Tickets come from Rare, Epic and Legendary Loot Crates - open it in the Nexus or your Guild Hall for your guild and party
- Fight waves of dungeon bosses from Easy to Apex, then Apex+ waves that grow stronger every time. Arena bosses drop their loot 1.5x more often (up to 1.75x)
- Heaven's Fall: the Lightwalk on the Blighted Causeway is much shorter (4 Spire stations instead of 8, the light moves faster)
- Key Vault: DISMANTLE a stored key into Key Fragments (Easy 2, Medium 5, Hard 8, Extreme 10)
- Bestiary: every rank now unlocks a perk - Fragment Finder, Crate Sense, Hunter's Share (half the damage share for boss loot), Fortune's Favor, Second Wind and, at Champion, Double Hunt plus the new Huntmaster aura
