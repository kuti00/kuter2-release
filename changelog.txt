Update 1.0.34
- New Extreme dungeon: the Godsmith's Vault - face Hoardlock in Thalor's lost treasure-labyrinth for new Godforged gear
- Godsmith Key: drops from Extreme bosses (and Skarnoth), forges from 25 Key Fragments and fits in the Key Vault
- Godforged abilities now grow with their level too
- Key Fragments no longer take bag space: bosses add them straight to your Key Vault, and double-clicking old fragments in your bags moves the whole stack there
- Legendary and Mythical gear drops twice as often in every spawner world (Easy to Infernal), including the Caldera and Abyssal side dungeons that open there
- Added a new anti-cheat system to keep the game fair for everyone
