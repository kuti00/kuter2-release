Update 1.0.36
- New companion rarity: Sovereign! Hellborn Imp, Hoardling, Seasonling and Lichling - minions of our hardest bosses, 3x a Celestial's bonus (a Striker 2x) and potions heal 100% more
- Sovereign companions drop wherever Celestial ones do (Extreme and Apex bosses, Public Golems, Wyrmling Eggs, Legendary Loot Crates), half as often
- Fame Key Merchant: Everwild and Godsmith keys (10,000 Fame) and the Hall of Judgement key (20,000 Fame) are now for sale
- Sigils buffed: Fortune +50% loot chance, Wrath +15% boss damage, Lineage +40% legacy, Hoard +20% Shards, Renown +50% boss Fame
- Heaven's Fall: the Lightwalk is half as long again (2 stations, the light moves twice as fast) and the blight burns harder; Blight Spires, the twin Gatekeepers and Malzareth hit much harder - the Demon King is now the deadliest fight
- Heaven's Fall: Blight Spires now drop fries instead of the Fame Crate - Rampart (50), Blighted (80), Seraph (100) and Demon King (150) Luck Rush XP
- Voidwrath Longbow: 4 arrows instead of 8 - all of them land on bosses now, so damage on bosses stays about the same
- Sealed Scarlet Bonecarver: 3 shards instead of 2, faster (Rate of Fire 45% -> 50%) and a little more range - now in line with the other Sealed Godforged weapons
- Bestiary: completed pages now glow - a gilded frame with twinkling sparkles and a SHEET COMPLETE ribbon, and their medallions shimmer in the boss strip
- Endless Arena: Apex+ waves now grow 10% stronger per wave instead of 15% (wave 25 is about 2.6x instead of 4x)
